Ordering and Delivery

Delivery rules, fees, areas, timing, and online ordering basics.

Need route-specific detail? Visit the delivery page or compare with collection.

The minimum order for delivery is £20.00. If your basket is below £20.00, you will need to add more items before you can place a delivery order. You can also choose collection, which has no minimum order.

The delivery fee is £2.50 per order. This is charged once per delivery, regardless of order size.

We deliver within a 4-mile radius of our kitchen at BD7 1BA. This covers postcodes BD1, BD2, BD3, BD4, BD5, BD6, BD7, BD8, BD9, BD10, BD11, and BD12. If you are unsure whether we deliver to your address, check the postcode list on our delivery page.

Delivery typically takes 30 to 45 minutes from when you place your order. During busy periods, such as Friday and Saturday evenings, delivery may take slightly longer.

Yes. You can collect from our kitchen at 8 Mannville Terrace, Bradford, BD7 1BA. Collection has no minimum order and no delivery fee. Your order will be ready in approximately 20 minutes.

Menu and Dietary

Halal standards, menu items, spice level, freshness, and allergens.

You can browse the full menu hub, plus future explainers for what is a smash burger and what is a gyro box.

Yes. Pollo Ranch is HMC halal certified. Every item on our menu uses halal-certified ingredients. HMC is one of the most respected halal certification bodies in the UK.

Yes. We offer a Veg Burrito (£5.99), a Veg Wrap (£4.99), a Veggy Burger (£4.99), and two vegetarian pizzas: Vegetarian and Spicy Vegetarian, both £8.99.

A smash burger is made by pressing a ball of fresh beef onto a hot griddle, which creates a crispy, caramelised crust on the outside while keeping the inside juicy. The Maillard reaction during cooking gives smash burgers their distinctive flavour. Read more on our What is a Smash Burger page.

A gyro lovers box contains your choice of gyro meat, served with warm pitta bread, fresh salad, and garlic or tzatziki sauce. Visit our What is a Gyro Box page for more detail.

We serve Classic Chicken, Classic Beef, Classic Mix, and Donner Stir Fry loaded fries, all £7.95. Every portion comes with nacho cheese, jalapenos and spicy mayo.

Our peri-peri chicken is medium heat. It has a noticeable warmth without being overwhelmingly hot. If you prefer something milder, ask for the standard seasoning when you order.

About Pollo Ranch

Location, opening hours, founding date, and food hygiene rating.
