Gyro Explained

A complete boxed meal built around sliced, seasoned rotisserie-style meat.

A gyro box is a complete meal based on gyro meat, a Greek and Middle Eastern style of cooking where seasoned meat is cooked on a vertical rotisserie and sliced thinly. A gyro box contains the sliced gyro meat, warm pitta bread, fresh salad, and a sauce such as garlic sauce or tzatziki.

Pollo Ranch serves halal gyro lovers boxes in Bradford with four options: chicken, beef, fish, and salmon.

What is Gyro Meat?

Seasoned, slowly cooked, and sliced from a vertical rotisserie.

Gyro meat is traditionally cooked on a vertical rotisserie. The meat is seasoned with spices, stacked onto the rotisserie, and cooked slowly as it rotates. Thin slices are cut from the outside of the rotating cone as it cooks.

The result is meat that is well-seasoned, slightly crisp on the outer edges, and tender on the inside. The flavour profile is savoury and spiced, with a texture that differs from grilled or fried meat.
