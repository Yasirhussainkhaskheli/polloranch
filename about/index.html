Since 2022

Built to give Bradford premium halal fast food from one trusted kitchen.

Pollo Ranch was incorporated on 7 April 2022. Since then, we have been serving Bradford with premium halal fast food, including stone-baked pizzas, smash burgers, fresh grilled chicken, burritos, gyro lovers boxes, and thick milkshakes.

We are based at 8 Mannville Terrace, Bradford, BD7 1BA, near the University of Bradford. We are open 11:40 until 00:45, seven days a week.

Our Food

Cooked to order, not held back under heat lamps.

Everything on our menu is halal certified and made fresh after you order. We do not pre-cook food and hold it under heat lamps. When you order, we cook.

Our pizzas are stone-baked. Our burgers are smash-style, pressed on a hot griddle. Our chicken is grilled fresh on a high-heat grill. Our burritos and wraps are assembled with fresh ingredients.

We designed the menu to give Bradford a single place where you can order quality halal pizza, quality halal burgers, and quality halal grilled chicken, all from the same kitchen.

Halal Certification

HMC halal certified from ingredients to kitchen standards.

Pollo Ranch is HMC halal certified. HMC stands for the Halal Monitoring Committee, one of the most respected halal certification bodies in the UK.

Every item on our menu uses halal-certified ingredients from suppliers we trust. Our certification covers all meat and poultry, and we do not use any non-halal products in our kitchen.

Food Hygiene Rating

5-star rated by the Food Standards Agency.

Pollo Ranch holds a 5-star Food Hygiene Rating from the Food Standards Agency. This is the highest possible rating and means our kitchen, food handling processes, and management systems have been inspected and meet the required standard.

Our Mission

Bradford deserves a premium halal takeaway that does not compromise on quality.

Pollo Ranch was built to fill that gap.

We keep our menu focused on dishes we can make well. We source fresh ingredients. We cook everything to order. That is the standard we hold ourselves to on every order.
